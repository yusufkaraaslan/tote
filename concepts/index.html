Tote, redrawn — 3 directions

Same app, three bets on what Tote should feel like. Open each, click around (tabs, spaces, ⌘K in B), and hit the ? why button in the corner of each page for the design rationale. Nothing here is final — pick what resonates and we'll talk.

A

Terminal Native

tmux/vim grammar — Tote stops pretending to be a web app.
  • one status line replaces strip + group bar
  • monospace, one amber accent, zero rounding
  • numbered panes, key-hint line

B

Command Center

AI OS — panes are floating cards, ⌘K is the interface.
  • glass cards over a gradient canvas
  • command palette drives everything
  • spaces as a centered pill switcher
open concept B →
C

Rail — light & calm

focused workbench — tabs over tiles, paper instead of neon.
  • spaces become colored glyphs on a rail
  • groups as a shelf of tabs
  • terminals stay dark inside a light UI
