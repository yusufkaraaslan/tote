Concept C — Rail (light & calm)

  • Idea: Tote as a focused workbench, not a mission control. Light paper UI, terminals stay dark — the contrast marks where agents live.
  • Spaces become colored glyphs on a rail (Arc/Notion muscle memory); name + path give the space an identity header.
  • Groups are literal tabs on a shelf; panes inside a group are tabs too — tiling is per-group, invisible until you split.
  • Density drops a lot; meant for one-pane-focus work with fast tab hops.
  • Question it tests: is Tote's power in simultaneous panes, or in fast switching? This concept bets on switching.
